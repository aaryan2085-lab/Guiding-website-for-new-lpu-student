Welcome, Freshers!

Starting university can be confusing. This FAQ guide helps newly admitted students find important information about admission, academics, UMS, campus facilities and student support in one place.

📋 1. Admission and Registration

How can I complete my admission process?

You can visit the official LPU admission website or physically visit the admission office. Follow the instructions provided by the university and submit all required documents. Visit LPU Admission Website

Which documents are required for admission?

Students generally need academic certificates, identity proof, photographs and admission-related documents. Check the official university instructions for the exact list.

How can I check my admission status?

Log in to the official student portal (UMS) or contact the admission department for updates.

Whom should I contact for registration problems?

Contact the LPU helpline at +91-1824-517000 or the admission department for assistance with registration-related problems.

📚 2. Academic Information

How can I check my class timetable?

Check your timetable through the LPU Touch mobile application or the University Management System (UMS).

Where can I find my course syllabus?

Students can check their course syllabus through the academic portal (UMS) or ask their faculty members.

How can I contact my faculty member?

Students can contact faculty members through official university communication channels such as LPU Live or during their available consultation hours.

Where can I check examination details?

Examination schedules and related announcements are generally available through the student portal or the LPU Touch application.

💻 3. UMS Portal

What is UMS?

UMS stands for University Management System. It provides access to important student and academic information.

How can I log in to UMS?

Open the official UMS portal and enter your university-provided login credentials.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the password recovery option if available or contact the university's IT support team.

What information is available on UMS?

Students can access relevant academic information, attendance, examination details and other university services, depending on their access.

🏫 4. Campus Tour

🌍 Explore LPU in 360°

Explore Lovely Professional University through its official virtual campus tour.

🌐 Visit Official LPU Website

Open the website and find the "Take a 360° Campus Tour" section.

Discover campus buildings, facilities and important university locations.

🏫 5. Campus Facilities

Where is the university library?

Students can ask the DSR office or the university help desk for directions to the library.

Are sports facilities available on campus?

Yes, students can explore the university sports facilities and check the rules for participation.

Where can I find the cafeteria?

Students can use the campus map or ask campus staff for directions to the cafeteria.

How can I find important campus locations?

Use the campus map and university information desk to locate important buildings and facilities.

🤝 6. Student Support and Guidance

Whom should I contact for academic difficulties?

Students can approach their faculty members, academic mentors or designated student support staff.

Is guidance available for new students?

Students can seek guidance from mentors, faculty members and the university student support team.

What should I do if I face a technical problem?

Contact the university IT help desk for assistance with technical problems.

Where can I get general student assistance?

Visit the university help desk or contact the relevant student support department.

⭐ Helpful Tips for Freshers

  • Keep your university ID card with you.
  • Check your timetable regularly.
  • Learn how to use the UMS portal.
  • Explore important campus locations.
  • Stay connected with faculty and classmates.
  • Ask for help whenever you face difficulties.

Need More Help?

For further assistance, contact the relevant university department or visit the official university help desk.

Stay confident and enjoy your university journey!
